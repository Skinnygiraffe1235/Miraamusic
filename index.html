About me

Miraamusic is a DJ, producer, and music engineering student based in Milan.

He's been behind the decks for ten years, and behind the sound for longer — he plays instruments, understands how a system actually reproduces sound, and handles his own production, mixing, and mastering.
Before Milan, he co-founded Balva, a private club in his hometown of Tehran — over a thousand subscribers, parties of 100+ people, and a team that ran everything themselves: DJing, sound system, marketing, ambience and acoustics that shaped the room. Zero to a hundred, on their own terms.

That same instinct — build it, tune it, run it — is carried into every set today.

For every hour of the night.
